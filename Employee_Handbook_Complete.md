## 2. EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

### Employment Classifications
- **Full-time employees**: Work 40+ hours per week with full benefits
- **Part-time employees**: Work less than 40 hours per week with prorated benefits
- **Contract employees**: Temporary workers with specific term agreements
- **Interns**: Students or recent graduates in learning programs

### At-Will Employment
Employment is at-will, meaning either the employee or company may terminate the relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice, subject to applicable laws.

### Background Checks
All job offers are contingent upon satisfactory completion of background checks, which may include criminal history, employment verification, and reference checks.

### Probationary Period
New employees serve a 90-day probationary period during which performance and fit are evaluated. During this time, either party may terminate employment without notice.

### Employment Verification
All employment verification requests must be directed to the Human Resources department. Only dates of employment and job titles will be confirmed without written authorization.

## 3. WORKPLACE CONDUCT & ETHICS

### Code of Conduct
All employees must conduct themselves professionally and ethically. This includes:
- Treating colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with respect
- Maintaining confidentiality of sensitive information
- Avoiding conflicts of interest
- Complying with all applicable laws and regulations

### Anti-Harassment Policy
We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation. This includes:
- Sexual harassment of any kind
- Verbal or physical abuse
- Discriminatory behavior based on protected characteristics
- Creating a hostile work environment

### Reporting Violations
Employees who witness or experience misconduct should report it immediately to:
- Direct supervisor
- Human Resources department
- Anonymous ethics hotline: 1-800-XXX-XXXX
- Email: ethics@company.com

### Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
Employees must protect confidential information including:
- Client data and trade secrets
- Financial information
- Personnel records
- Strategic business plans
- Proprietary technology and processes

### Social Media Policy
When using social media:
- Do not share confidential company information
- Clearly state that views are your own, not the company's
- Maintain professional standards
- Report any concerning social media activity to HR

## 4. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

### Pay Periods & Payroll
- Bi-weekly pay periods (every other Friday)
- Direct deposit is required for all employees
- Pay stubs are available electronically
- Questions about pay should be directed to Payroll

### Overtime Policy
- Non-exempt employees receive overtime pay (1.5x regular rate) for hours worked over 40 in a workweek
- Overtime must be pre-approved by supervisors
- Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay

### Performance Reviews
- Annual performance reviews for all employees
- Mid-year check-ins with supervisors
- Performance ratings affect salary adjustments and promotions
- Goal setting and development planning included

### Health Insurance
We offer comprehensive health insurance including:
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Multiple plan options to fit different needs
- Company contributes 80% of premium costs
- Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment

### Retirement Benefits
- 401(k) plan with company matching up to 4% of salary
- Immediate vesting for employee contributions
- Company match vests after 2 years of service
- Financial planning resources available

### Additional Benefits
- Life insurance (2x annual salary, company-paid)
- Short-term and long-term disability insurance
- Employee assistance program (EAP)
- Flexible spending accounts (FSA)
- Transportation subsidies
- Employee discounts program

## 5. TIME OFF & LEAVE POLICIES

### Paid Time Off (PTO)
PTO accrues based on length of service:
- Years 1-2: 15 days annually (1.25 days per month)
- Years 3-5: 20 days annually (1.67 days per month)
- Years 6+: 25 days annually (2.08 days per month)

### PTO Guidelines
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
- Supervisor approval required
- Maximum carryover: 5 days to the following year
- PTO cannot be negative; unpaid leave required if insufficient balance

### Sick Leave
- 10 days annually for illness, medical appointments, or caring for sick family members
- No advance approval required for emergency sick leave
- Medical documentation may be required for absences over 3 consecutive days
- Unused sick leave does not carry over

### Holidays
We observe the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Columbus Day
- Veterans Day
- Thanksgiving Day and Friday
- Christmas Eve and Christmas Day

### Family & Medical Leave (FMLA)
Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid leave for:
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Serious health condition of employee or family member
- Military family leave
- Job protection and benefit continuation provided during leave

### Bereavement Leave
- 3 days paid leave for immediate family members
- 1 day paid leave for extended family members
- Additional unpaid leave may be available
- Documentation may be required

### Jury Duty
- Paid leave for jury duty service
- Must provide court documentation
- Any jury fees received must be remitted to the company

## 6. HEALTH & SAFETY

### Workplace Safety
We are committed to providing a safe work environment:
- Report all accidents and injuries immediately
- Follow all safety procedures and protocols
- Use personal protective equipment when required
- Attend mandatory safety training sessions

### Emergency Procedures
In case of emergency:
- **Fire**: Exit via nearest stairwell, gather at designated meeting point
- **Medical Emergency**: Call 911, notify security and HR
- **Severe Weather**: Move to interior rooms away from windows
- **Evacuation**: Follow posted evacuation routes and procedures

### Workers' Compensation
- All work-related injuries must be reported within 24 hours
- Medical treatment through approved providers
- Return-to-work programs available
- No retaliation for filing legitimate claims

### Workplace Violence Prevention
- Zero tolerance for violence or threats of violence
- Report suspicious behavior immediately
- Security escorts available
- Employee assistance program for support

### Drug & Alcohol Policy
- Zero tolerance for illegal drug use or alcohol impairment
- Pre-employment and reasonable suspicion testing
- Employee assistance programs for substance abuse
- Prescription medications must not impair work performance

## 9. TECHNOLOGY & EQUIPMENT

### Computer & Equipment Use
- Company equipment is for business use primarily
- Limited personal use permitted during breaks
- No expectation of privacy on company systems
- Report technical issues to IT support immediately

### Email & Internet Policy
- Professional communication standards apply
- No personal use that interferes with work duties
- Prohibited activities include gambling, adult content, illegal downloads
- All communications subject to monitoring

### Data Security
- Strong passwords required (8+ characters, mixed case, numbers)
- Password changes every 90 days
- No sharing of login credentials
- Secure sensitive data according to classification levels
- Report security incidents immediately

### Remote Work Technology
For remote workers:
- VPN access required for company systems
- Secure Wi-Fi connections only
- Company laptop and equipment provided
- Home office setup guidelines available

### Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
If using personal devices for work:
- Must install approved security software
- Company may remotely wipe business data
- Personal data kept separate from business data
- Agreement required before access granted

## 10. GRIEVANCE & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### Open Door Policy
We encourage open communication:
- Speak with your immediate supervisor first
- If unresolved, escalate to next management level
- HR department available for sensitive issues
- No retaliation for good faith concerns

### Formal Grievance Process
1. **Step 1**: Written complaint to supervisor within 10 days
2. **Step 2**: Appeal to department manager within 5 days
3. **Step 3**: Final review by HR Director within 5 days
4. **Documentation**: All steps documented in employee file

### Alternative Dispute Resolution
- Mediation services available for workplace conflicts
- Neutral third-party facilitators
- Voluntary participation encouraged
- Confidential process

### Retaliation Prohibition
- No retaliation for good faith reporting
- Protected activities include filing complaints, participating in investigations
- Retaliation violations subject to discipline up to termination
- Anonymous reporting options available

## 12. COMPLIANCE & LEGAL

### Anti-Corruption Policy
- No bribes, kickbacks, or improper payments
- Gifts and entertainment must be modest and infrequent
- Report any requests for improper payments
- Third-party due diligence required

### Data Privacy
- Comply with all applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Collect only necessary personal information
- Secure storage and transmission of personal data
- Individual rights regarding personal data honored

### Regulatory Compliance
- Stay current with industry regulations
- Required training completion tracked
- Regular compliance audits conducted
- Violations reported to appropriate authorities

### Document Retention
- Business records retained per legal requirements
- Litigation hold procedures when applicable
- Secure destruction of expired documents
- Electronic and physical records included

### Insider Trading
- No trading on material non-public information
- Blackout periods for certain employees
- Pre-clearance required for executive trades
- Report suspicious trading activity
